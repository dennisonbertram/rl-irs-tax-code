## 14. Education Credits

### American Opportunity Tax Credit (AOTC)
- Maximum credit: $2,500 per eligible student (all years)
- Refundable portion: 40% (up to $1,000)
- Available for first 4 years of post-secondary education

### Lifetime Learning Credit (LLC)
- Maximum credit: $2,000 per return (all years)
- Non-refundable

### MAGI Phaseout (both credits share the same phaseout for 2025)

| Filing Status | Phaseout Begins | Phaseout Complete |
|---|---|---|
| Single / HOH | $80,000 | $90,000 |
| MFJ | $160,000 | $180,000 |

Note: These phaseout amounts are not inflation-indexed. The $2,500 AOTC and $2,000 LLC maximums are statutory and not adjusted for inflation.

Source: IRC 25A

## 16. Section 1031 Like-Kind Exchange

- **Dollar limit**: None. There is no cap on the amount of gain that may be deferred.
- **Reporting**: Form 8824 must be filed for the year of the exchange and for subsequent years if the transaction involves related parties.
- **Scope**: Limited to real property held for productive use in a trade or business or for investment (post-TCJA).

Source: IRC 1031; Reg. 1.1031

## Caveats

1. **OBBBA (One Big Beautiful Bill Act)**: Signed in 2025, this legislation changed several provisions effective for tax years beginning after December 31, 2024 or 2025. Key changes include: increased child tax credit, extended TCJA provisions, expanded Section 179 limits, and modifications to the QBI deduction. Some 2025 figures in this dataset reflect pre-OBBBA amounts from Rev. Proc. 2024-40; consult the final enacted legislation for any OBBBA adjustments.

2. **Education credit phaseouts**: The AOTC and LLC phaseout thresholds ($80K/$90K single, $160K/$180K MFJ) are statutory and have not been adjusted for inflation.

3. **IRA catch-up**: The $1,000 IRA catch-up contribution is statutory (IRC 219(b)(5)(B)) and not indexed for inflation.

4. **Child Tax Credit phaseouts**: The $200,000/$400,000 phaseout thresholds are not indexed for inflation.
